## 1.0.0 (2026-10-28)

First public version.

- Idea cycle: resting, review, ripening, growing, harvested or composted, with a date for every stage.
- Required rest period before review (7 days by default) and limited growing slots (1 to 3, 3 by default).
- Logo: four squares, three growing places and one field left to rest, filled as ideas grow.
- Four essential questions before growing, and a step-back question on every idea.
- One-screen interface: tabs, two panes from 760 px wide, bottom bar and new-idea sheet on phones.
- Quiet by default: filters appear only once a tab holds 5 ideas; empty tabs show a dashed plot that invites the next idea.
- Plain English wording: "Add", "Added", "Start again" instead of farming verbs.
- "Today" view: month figures, reviews, overdue harvests, next dates.
- Timeline view of all dates.
- French and English interface.
- Obsidian export and import (Markdown notes with properties, Dataview dashboard, note template).
- Printable A6 cards.
- Installable app (manifest, icons, service worker): "Install Novale" button in the options, iPhone instructions, "New idea" shortcut on the app icon, works offline, asks the browser to keep the data persistent. No network requests.
- "Delete everything" in the options, with three confirmations (the last one asks you to type a word).
